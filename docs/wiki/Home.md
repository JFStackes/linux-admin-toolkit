## Design goals

1. Make frequent Linux administration tasks faster without hiding the underlying system.
2. Keep scripts readable and auditable.
3. Use safe defaults and explicit confirmation for destructive actions.
4. Keep secrets out of logs.
5. Provide consistent JFStack output, reports and documentation.
